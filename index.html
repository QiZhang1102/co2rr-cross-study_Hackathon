02 / KNOW WHAT THE MODEL KNOWS

A useful suggestion.
Not a promise.

NextExperiment uses established Gaussian process regression and expected improvement. Each task fits its own observations. It does not use a language model or transfer a CO₂RR model to your data.

Explore the original research ↗
What does uncertainty mean?

“Model SD” is the model's estimated standard deviation in target units. It is not experimental error or a validated probability of success. Sparse data and model assumptions can make it misleading. Predictions are not clipped to physical limits.

Where do the public results come from?

Li et al., Nature Catalysis (2023), 10.1038/s41929-023-01046-8. Numerical facts were checked against printed mean labels in Figures 2c, 3a and 3c. The two potential-reference cohorts remain separate. No raw replicates are available here. Figures and PDFs are not redistributed.

What can this prototype establish?

Only selection within a finite list of candidates. A replay reveals historical measurements; it does not perform a new experiment. A small pool or a single seed cannot establish general superiority, improved yield, or cost savings. Alternatives are individual substitutes, not an optimized batch.

Data rules & privacy

Use only public or simulated data, with consistent units, experimental context and potential references. No patient data, diagnosis or treatment advice. Files and calculations stay in this browser. Reloading clears your task; export before leaving. Optional anonymous event records are stored on this device only. Hosting providers may retain standard access logs.
